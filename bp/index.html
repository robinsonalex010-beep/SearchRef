Record your readings here, then send the summary to your GP practice.

Your readings are saved on this phone only, in this web browser. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to send the summary. Use the same phone and browser each time, and don’t use private browsing, or your readings won’t be kept.

How to take your blood pressure
  1. Don’t smoke, have caffeine or exercise for 30 minutes before.
  2. Sit in a chair with your back supported and your feet flat on the floor. Rest your arm on a table so the cuff is at the level of your heart.
  3. Sit quietly for 5 minutes without talking.
  4. Take a reading, wait 1 minute, then take a second reading. Enter both.
  5. Do this twice a day, morning and evening, for 7 days if you can (at least 4 days).
